The built-in fonts hershey_simplex.shx and hershey_duplex.shx were converted
by cadrender (tools/hershey_to_shx.py) from the Hershey font data files
rowmans.jhf (Roman Simplex) and rowmand.jhf (Roman Duplex). Accented letters
and the symbols degree, plus-minus, diameter, superscripts and middle dot
were added by cadrender.

Acknowledgements required by the Hershey font distribution:

- The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while
  working at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
- The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by
  James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821.

The use restriction of that distribution says the data may be used by anyone
for any purpose, commercial or otherwise, as long as the acknowledgements
above are distributed with the font data, and that the data may be converted
into any other format except the format distributed by the U.S. NTIS.
